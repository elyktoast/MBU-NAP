## Decision rule
- REMOVE when the image is a lecture slide/screenshot that repeats the stem, exposes the answer, or is not needed to solve the item.
- KEEP + CROP/DE-LABEL only when visual interpretation, identification, waveform/graph reading, or spatial anatomy materially contributes to the question.
- A figure may be removed from most questions but retained in a small number of genuinely visual items.
