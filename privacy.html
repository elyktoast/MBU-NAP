Privacy Notice

Effective: September 27, 2026

SNAR Study Tool is an independent educational study resource. It is not operated by, affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any university, nursing program, faculty member, certification body, or licensing organization.

Guest use

Most study features can be used without an account. Guest progress is stored locally in the browser. SNAR Study Tool does not intentionally submit guest question-performance data to the calibration database.

Account and Adaptive Mode data

An account is required for Adaptive Mode and may also be used for cross-device synchronization. Account authentication is provided through Supabase and requires an email address and authentication credentials. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored in the study application's question-performance records.

For signed-in users, SNAR Study Tool may process question identifiers, whether a first attempt was correct, optional response time, session mode, study progress, synchronization metadata, and a private account identifier. The private account identifier is used to associate an account with its own progress and to prevent the same account from contributing multiple first-attempt observations for the same question.

Testing and calibration

Question-performance information may be used for testing, validation, question-quality improvement, and Adaptive Mode calibration. Population calibration results are de-identified and aggregated. The aggregate calibration dataset does not include names, email addresses, passwords, or device identity.

De-identification commitment: SNAR Study Tool will maintain de-identified calibration data in de-identified form, will not attempt to re-identify it, will not sell it, and will not use it for targeted advertising.

Data collected and purposes

  • Email and authentication data: account creation, sign-in, account security, password recovery, and session management.
  • Study progress: resume sessions, show performance history, Smart Review, Due Review, backups, and cross-device synchronization.
  • First-attempt performance: prevent duplicate statistical contributions, test question quality, and improve Adaptive Mode calibration.
  • Technical synchronization metadata: maintain reliable cloud synchronization, version history, and device-management features.

SNAR Study Tool does not intentionally collect sensitive demographic, health, financial, precise-geolocation, or government-identification information for Adaptive Mode calibration.

Service providers

Supabase is used for account authentication, database storage, and synchronization. GitHub Pages is used to host the website. Those providers may process technical information necessary to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

Sale, advertising, and profiling

SNAR Study Tool does not sell personal data and does not use personal data for targeted advertising. Adaptive question selection is a study feature and is not used to make decisions about education enrollment, employment, insurance, lending, health care, or other legally significant eligibility decisions.

Retention and deletion

Local guest progress remains in the browser until the user clears it or imports/replaces it. Signed-in progress is retained while the account remains active or until it is replaced or deleted through available controls. Account deletion removes the authentication account and account-linked cloud records through database deletion rules. The affected aggregate calibration statistics are recalculated from remaining contributions.

De-identified aggregate statistics may be retained after account deletion because they are maintained without identifying the deleted user.

Your controls

Signed-in users can download a backup of their study progress, manage synchronized devices, restore prior progress, sign out, and delete their account and associated account-linked data from the account panel. These controls are the primary method for accessing and deleting data held by SNAR Study Tool.

Age

SNAR Study Tool is intended for adult learners. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use Adaptive Mode. The service is not directed to children under 13.

Security

SNAR Study Tool uses authenticated access controls, row-level security where applicable, restricted database functions, and data minimization. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Educational and research status

Performance data is collected for operation, testing, validation, and improvement of the study tool. It is not represented as university-sponsored human-subject research. If the project is later used for formal research intended for publication or generalizable research conclusions, the operator should obtain any required institutional or ethics review before using data for that purpose.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when data practices or features change. Materially different uses of personal data should be disclosed before the new use begins and, where legally required, appropriate consent should be obtained.

This notice describes the current technical design and is not a substitute for advice from a licensed attorney regarding a particular deployment or jurisdiction.
