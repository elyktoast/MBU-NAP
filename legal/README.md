# SNAR Study Tool Legal & Compliance Operations

This directory contains the operational records that support the Terms of Use and Privacy Notice.

- `versions/` contains immutable legal-document snapshots and hash manifests.
- `LEGAL_CHANGELOG.md` records material legal/privacy changes.
- `INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md` defines the security-incident workflow.
- `RETENTION_SCHEDULE.md` defines data retention and disposal periods.
- `DATA_INVENTORY.md` documents data types, purposes, storage, access, and deletion.
- `PROVIDERS.md` identifies operational service providers and the limited purposes for which they are used.
- `ADMIN_OPERATIONS.md` documents the operator-admin role and least-privilege procedures.

These documents are operational controls for the current deployment. They should be reviewed whenever collection, providers, authentication, Adaptive Mode, research use, or school/clinical relationships materially change.
